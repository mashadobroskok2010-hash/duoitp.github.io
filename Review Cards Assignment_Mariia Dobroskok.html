Economics Review Cards

What is inflation?
A sustained increase in the general price level.
What is GDP?
The total value of goods and services produced in a country.
What is unemployment?
People able and willing to work but unable to find jobs.
What is demand?
The quantity consumers are willing and able to buy.
What is supply?
The quantity producers are willing and able to sell.
What is a trade union?
An organization that represents workers' interests.
What is monetary policy?
Actions by a central bank to control money supply and interest rates.
What is fiscal policy?
Government spending and taxation used to influence the economy.
