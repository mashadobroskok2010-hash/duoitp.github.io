How to Embed a YouTube Video

Step 1

Open YouTube and choose a video.

Step 2

Click the Share button under the video.

Step 3

Select Embed option.

Step 4

Copy the iframe code.

Step 5

Paste the code into your HTML file.

Step 6

Save and open your page in the browser.
